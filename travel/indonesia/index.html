Several journeys in one

Travelling through Indonesia is less like visiting many different places than making several different journeys. From the deranged city of Jakarta, with its brutal contrasts, to the great Buddhist and Hindu temple complexes; from diving with sharks in Komodo to spending three days travelling upriver in Borneo, in a kind of family Heart of Darkness, with only orangutans for company. From Bali’s terrifying figures to the markets of Semarang.

Indonesia does not feel like a single country crossed from end to end, but several journeys that, somehow, happened one after another.
