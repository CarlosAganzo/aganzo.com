THREE FIXED POINTS

Places that arrived before the map.

Some destinations become important long before you reach them.

01 / FIRST OBSESSION

Loch Ness

The first place I ever remember wanting to travel to. The monster helped; Tintin and The Black Island probably helped more. Years later I ended up living in northern Britain, unexpectedly close to that childhood coordinate.

02 / RETURN

Patagonia

I have only been once. I would go back a hundred times. Some places remain destinations; Patagonia became a reference point.

03 / STILL TO COME

Himalaya

The one still missing. I have flown over it and watched it through aircraft windows several times, but I have never yet stood there.
