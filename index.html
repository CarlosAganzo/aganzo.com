A few things.
In considerable detail.

I'm from Madrid, although I’ve lived in the UK for a long time now. I still carry quite a lot of my city with me: the dry humour, the impatience with anything too grand, and a certain affection for things that are a little rough around the edges.

I tend to be drawn to the same qualities in music, films and places. Things with some darkness in them, things that leave room for interpretation, and things that can be melancholy without trying too hard to be moving. I’m probably more interested than I should be in memory, passing time, and the strange way places and songs end up attached to particular versions of ourselves.

Most of what is on this site comes from that: places I’ve been, photographs I wanted to keep, music and films that stayed with me, and the occasional attempt to understand why.

WORK

Also, software.

Low-level by instinct. Generalist by management.

I wanted to work with computers from about the age of nine — roughly when I gave up on becoming an astronaut. By university I was already making demoscene-style demos, writing software for the family business, and taking on small commercial projects for companies on my own.

As a software engineer, I naturally drifted towards the lower layers: data access, systems work, banking and payments — more interested in the machinery underneath applications than in the applications themselves.

Management broadened that considerably: mobile software for managed service providers, analytics and business intelligence, payments at Sage, product engineering, and eventually the shared platforms underneath multiple products. Today I have management responsibility across several engineering teams and technical domains.
