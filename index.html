I build systems.
I collect obsessions.

From Madrid. Based in Newcastle. Engineering leader, film obsessive, music lover and traveller with a camera.

I like things that are built properly, whether they are software platforms, cameras, records, films, clothes or plans for getting from one place to another.

01

WORK

Engineering leadership without theatre.

I work on the less glamorous parts of software that become very glamorous the moment they fail: identity, authentication, shared services, messaging and platform infrastructure.

My job is mostly about making complicated systems simpler, helping teams make good decisions, and being comfortable saying “I don’t know” before finding out.

I lead engineering teams at N-able, working across the UK and Romania. My focus is the common ground beneath different products: how people sign in, how services communicate, and how teams build on infrastructure they can trust.

OUTSIDE WORK

Walking, looking, listening.

Photography

Small cameras, available light, streets, landscapes and the kind of pictures that are better because nobody noticed they were being taken.

Travel

From the coast of North Yorkshire to Indonesia: walking the Cleveland Way, travelling through Java, and seeing Borneo from a boat. I like a trip with several chapters, and enough time to actually look around.

Cinema, in large doses.

I watch around 200 films a year, a habit that goes back to my teenage years. Paris, Texas, Drive, The Straight Story and The Assassination of Jesse James are good coordinates for my taste. I have a soft spot for horror, too.

Collections

I collect Pokémon cards with a particular interest in the artwork of Tomokazu Komiya and Shinji Kanda. The illustration is what draws me in: strange compositions, unexpected colour, and a recognisable hand.

SIDE B / 33⅓ RPM

Bowie, Radiohead, Massive Attack, Interpol, Sonic Youth, Queens of the Stone Age and Los Planetas. Then Otis Redding or Neil Young. Different sounds, but the songs that stay with me tend to have a little darkness in them.

ABOUT

Madrid → North East England.

Spanish by origin, British by weather exposure. I have spent much of my adult life in the North East of England and still regard Madrid as home in a way that geography does not entirely explain.

I grew up in Madrid and have lived in Newcastle upon Tyne for around fifteen years. I like having both worlds in my life: the place I come from and the place where I have built a different kind of everyday life.
