02 / Plan B · Saturday 21

Cool alternatives.

Two ideas for after Frameless if we decide not to buy Paranormal Activity tickets. Neither is booked.

PLAN B1 · FREE · NO BOOKING

Southbank Winter Market

Winter chalets, street food, lights and the Thames after dark. Stroll along Queen's Walk beneath Hungerford Bridge, with views towards Parliament and the City.

17:00–19:15 · suggested

Stalls 11:00–22:00; bars until 23:00. Free admission; food and drinks extra. From Frameless: Central line from Marble Arch to Tottenham Court Road, then walk towards Charing Cross and cross the pedestrian bridge (around 35–45 min).

PLAN B2 · OFFBEAT COMEDY · 14+

Frankie Thompson: Horrible Things

Clowning, performance and absurd, uncomfortable comedy. The final show of the run at Soho Theatre, 21 Dean Street. A cult alternative to stage horror.

21:00–22:00 · Saturday 21

Advertised prices: £18, £21 or £24 seated; £15 standing. Three seats: £54–72, subject to availability. 60 minutes; recommended age 14+. May contain flashing lights, haze and loud noises.
